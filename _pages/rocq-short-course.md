## Running/Installing Rocq Theorem Prover

**Remote running (jscoq)**

You can have a limited setup to run simple examples and test basic tactics online.
It can be useful for the first lecture.

[https://jscoq.github.io/scratchpad.html](https://jscoq.github.io/scratchpad.html)

**Local Setup**

For a more robust interfacing, it is recommended to have it installed locally.
The official Rocq page, i.e., [https://rocq-prover.org/install](https://rocq-prover.org/install#windows-vscode) contains specific installations for your platform, including Windows.

During the lectures, we will use ``vscode`` which I recommend for its simplicity.
